# Product overview

EstimateIn10 Field Service is a standalone local development app for reviewing contractor jobs, scheduling existing service visits, and recording completed work. It includes fictional customers, properties, equipment, and team members.

## Current capabilities

- A technician-grouped schedule and job detail views.
- Rescheduling, visit status changes, and completion notes.
- Owner and technician role checks, scheduling conflict protection, and an activity log.
- A read-only equipment register and persistent local PostgreSQL storage.

## Planned development

Advanced dispatch planning, creation of follow-up visits, equipment service history, and recurring maintenance remain future work.

The demonstration user selector is for local fictional-data development, not production authentication. All runtime data and services stay on the local computer.
